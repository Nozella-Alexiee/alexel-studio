# Code of conduct

We want Alexel Studio to be a friendly project for everyone, whatever their experience,
background or identity.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive. Help newcomers.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and
may block people who repeatedly do so. Report problems privately to the maintainers through
the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
